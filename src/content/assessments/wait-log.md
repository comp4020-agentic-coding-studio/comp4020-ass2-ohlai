## The brief

> Log one week of your own waits over ten seconds. For each, record the clock
> duration, the felt duration, and what you were told while it happened.

The instrument is the one you build in the week 1 workshop. The third column
is the one people leave blank, and it is the one this assignment is about. A
wait where you were told nothing and a wait where you were told something are
different waits, and your own numbers will show you that before any reading
does.

Do not clean the data. A log with two entries you cannot explain is worth
more than a tidy one, and the 500 words are where you say which entries those
are.

## What you submit

The log, in any legible format, and 500 words. Name at least one wait where
the clock duration and the felt duration moved in opposite directions, and
say what was in the third column when that happened.

This tests the first clause of the thesis, that waiting is a designed
component and not a failure. You are looking for the design in waits nobody
ever described to you as designed.
