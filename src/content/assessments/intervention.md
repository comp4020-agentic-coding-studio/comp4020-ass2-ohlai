## The brief

> Redesign a wait you are not allowed to make shorter.

The constraint is the assignment. Anyone can propose making it faster, and
the proposal is usually somebody else's budget. You work with the duration
fixed, which leaves you the whole of the rest of this course and nothing
else.

Say what makes it unshortenable: a regulatory hold, a physical process, a
batch window, a queue with a fixed service rate. If you cannot name the
constraint then you have chosen a wait that could simply be shortened, and
you should choose again.

## What you submit

Before and after, at enough fidelity that someone could build the after.
Then a justification that does two things. It names the technique you used
from weeks 5 to 8, and it names the person that technique acts on.

The second half is the assignment. Every technique in this course works by
managing somebody, and the ones that work best manage somebody who never
agreed to it. Write down who that is for your intervention. A justification
that cannot name them has not finished.

This tests the third clause, that who is made to wait, for how long, and
what they are told, is never neutral. You are marked on whether you can hold
that and design something anyway.
