---
title: The bar is not the process
description: >-
  Determinate progress, where the system commits to a number it does not
  have, and the design space that opens once you accept that.
week: 4
date: 2027-03-09
---

A progress bar makes a promise a system is usually in no position to keep. It
has to name a fraction before it knows the denominator, so it estimates, and
then it has to keep the estimate moving while the estimate changes.

Once you accept that the bar is not a readout of the process, it becomes a
piece of design with its own variables. Harrison and colleagues changed how
long a fixed duration felt by changing only how the bar animated. The
underlying process was identical in every condition.
