Up to now the course has treated a wait as something that happens to one
person. A queue is different. It puts you next to other people and gives you
something to compare yourself against, and from that point the wait is about
the comparison at least as much as the duration.

Larson made the argument that people are angered less by delay than by
violation of order. The lecture takes that seriously enough to ask what a
queue is for, given that a single line into many servers is usually slower
per person than the alternatives and is chosen anyway.
