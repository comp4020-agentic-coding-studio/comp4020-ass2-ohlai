---
title: Showing the work
description: >-
  Operational transparency, and the finding that people can prefer a slower
  service that lets them watch it work.
week: 5
date: 2027-03-16
---

The labour illusion is where this course stops being about perception and
starts being about persuasion. Buell and Norton found people valuing a search
more highly when it took longer and showed its work, with the results held
identical across conditions.

The lecture is about what that licenses. Once showing effort raises perceived
value, effort becomes something to display rather than something to spend,
and a display can be manufactured.
