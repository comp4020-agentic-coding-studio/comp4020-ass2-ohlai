  Duration neglect and the peak-end rule, and why the last minute of a wait
  is worth more than the middle of it.
week: 11
date: 2027-05-11
---

The wait you report is not the wait you had. Redelmeier and Kahneman
measured pain in real time during medical procedures and then asked people
afterwards, and what people reported tracked the worst moment and the final
moments, with the length of the procedure barely registering.

If that holds for waiting, and the course argues it does, then the end of a
wait is worth disproportionately more than the middle. The lecture is about
what follows from that, including the parts you should be uneasy about.
