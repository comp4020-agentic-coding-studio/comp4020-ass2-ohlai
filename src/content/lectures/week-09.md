This is the lecture where the course stops being about interfaces. Waiting is
not evenly distributed, and the distribution is not an accident of demand. It
is produced, and it can be read.

Auyero spent years in welfare waiting rooms and argues that what the poor
learn while waiting is the opposite of citizenship. Schwartz got there in
1975 from sociology, treating delay as a marker of relative standing. Neither
of them is talking about a spinner, and both of them are talking about your
Intervention.
