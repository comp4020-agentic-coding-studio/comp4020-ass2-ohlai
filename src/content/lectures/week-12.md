title: Defending a wait
description: >-
  The closing lecture, on what it means to design a wait you are not allowed
  to shorten, and who you answer to when you do.
week: 12
date: 2027-05-18
---

The course ends where it started, with a wait nobody can make shorter. What
has changed is the number of things you can now do to it, and your obligation
to say which person each of them acts on.

Sharma is the closing frame. She argues that both speeding up and slowing
down are ways of arranging other people in time, and that the interesting
question is never the pace but whose pace is being maintained by whom. Take
that into the studio and point it at your own intervention.
