---
title: The genre of the hold
description: >-
  What you are given during a wait, treated as authored material with a
  history, a genre and an economics behind it.
week: 8
date: 2027-04-20
---

Somebody wrote the message you hear on hold. Somebody licensed the music
under it, chose its tempo, and decided how often the loop reminds you that
your call is important. None of that is incidental and all of it is
purchasable.

The lecture reads the hold as a composed object. Sterne is the model for that
reading: he treats programmed music in a shopping mall as part of the
building rather than as decoration in it, and the same move works on a phone
queue.
