title: Delay on purpose
description: >-
  Latency added rather than removed, and the line between a deception that
  serves the person and one that serves the system.
week: 10
date: 2027-05-04
---

Every week so far has assumed the delay was there and the question was what
to do about it. This lecture removes that assumption. Systems add delay on
purpose, and some of the reasons are good ones.

Adar, Tan and Teevan give the category a name and a defence. Brignull
catalogues the same techniques where the beneficiary is the other party. The
lecture puts the two side by side and refuses to give you a rule for telling
them apart, because there is not one that survives contact with a real case.
