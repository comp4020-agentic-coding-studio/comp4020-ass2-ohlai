---
title: Filling the time
description: >-
  The techniques that consume attention during a wait rather than reducing
  it, and the question of what that attention is worth.
week: 6
date: 2027-03-23
---

Occupied time is the oldest idea in this course and the one with the widest
practical use. If unoccupied time feels longer, then giving a waiting person
something to do is an intervention available at almost no cost.

The lecture catalogues the techniques and then asks the uncomfortable
question, which is who the occupation is for. Time filled on your behalf is
time you have given away without being asked.
