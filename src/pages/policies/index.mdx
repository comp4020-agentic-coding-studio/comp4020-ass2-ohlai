# Policies and support

## When marks are released

Marks for this course are released in week 12. Not for individual pieces of
work as they are marked, and not on a rolling basis. All of it, in week 12.

This is a deliberate delay and it is part of the course. Week 11 is on
duration neglect and the peak-end rule, which is the research on why the end
of an experience carries more weight in memory than its middle. You will be
reading that material while waiting for your own results, and you are invited
to argue with the policy using it.

Two things this policy is not. It is not a claim that the delay is good for
you, which is the argument every withheld thing makes about itself. And it is
not negotiable individually, because a queue that moves for whoever asks is
the failure week 7 is about.

If you need a mark released earlier for a documented reason, such as a
scholarship or a visa deadline, email the convenor with the deadline and
evidence of it. That exception is granted on the document rather than on the
request.

A day is a whole day from the due time, so an hour late and twenty-three
hours late cost the same. Once an item has reached the floor, the
conversation to have is about the course rather than the piece.

## Extensions

Ask before the due time.

The turnaround is published because it is the course's own wait. This course
does not get to argue that delay is designed and then leave you refreshing
your inbox for an answer it has already reached. If the turnaround passes
without a decision, send the email again and say it is the second one.

The one exception already decided is above. If a documented external deadline
requires your final mark before week 12, that is granted on the document.

## Academic integrity

Your work is your own. Where you used a tool, a collaborator or a source that
shaped the work, say so in the submission. The Wait Log in particular is data
you collected, and fabricated entries are the one thing in this course that
cannot be argued into being interesting.

## Getting help

Ask in the workshop first, because most questions are shared and answering
one in the room answers it for everybody who had it.

For anything that does not fit a workshop, there is consultation. The
convenor is available Wednesdays from 14:00 to 15:30 during teaching weeks.
The tutor stays for thirty minutes after each Thursday workshop, in the
workshop room.

Both are drop-in. Neither takes bookings, because a booking system is a queue
with an admission test on the front of it, and week 7 is about what that
does to the people who fail the test.
