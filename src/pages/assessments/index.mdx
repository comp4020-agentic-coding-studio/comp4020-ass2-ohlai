Four pieces of work. Each one tests a named clause of the course thesis, and
each page says which.

The weighting is deliberate. The Intervention is worth 45 because it is the
only piece that asks you to design under a constraint you cannot remove, and
most of its marks sit in the justification rather than the artefact.
