# Two durations, one wait

SLOP2034. Week 1.

---

## A wait has two durations

The clock gives one.

The person gives the other.

They are rarely the same number.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Only one of them is designable

---

## What this course is not

It is not a course about making things faster.

Every technique after week 2 leaves the clock alone.

---

## The thesis

> Waiting is not a failure of a system, it is a designed component of it.

The rest of the line arrives in weeks 5 and 9. Read it on the front page
before then.

---

## Three things you will do

- log a week of your own waits
- take one shipped wait apart
- redesign a wait you are not allowed to shorten

The third one is the course.

---

## Why the log comes first

You will read that felt duration and clock duration diverge.

Reading it takes a minute and convinces nobody.

Measuring it on yourself takes a week and is harder to argue with
afterwards.

---

## The third column

Clock duration. Felt duration. What you were told.

Most people leave the third blank on their first attempt, and then cannot
explain their own numbers.

---
